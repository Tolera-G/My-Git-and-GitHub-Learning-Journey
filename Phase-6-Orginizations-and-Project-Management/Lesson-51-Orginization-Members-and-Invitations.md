# Lesson 51: Organization Members and Invitations

## Overview

GitHub Organizations allow multiple developers to collaborate under a shared workspace.

Organization owners can invite developers, manage membership, review pending invitations, and remove members when necessary.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand organization membership.
- Invite developers to an organization.
- Accept organization invitations.
- Understand pending invitations.
- Remove organization members.
- Differentiate members from outside collaborators.

## 1. Organization Membership

An organization member is a GitHub user who has accepted an invitation to join an organization.

Members use their personal GitHub accounts to collaborate.

Being an organization member does not automatically provide write access to every repository.

## 2. Inviting Members

To invite a developer:

1. Open the GitHub Organization.
2. Navigate to People.
3. Select Invite member.
4. Enter the developer's GitHub username or email.
5. Choose the appropriate membership role.
6. Send the invitation.

## 3. Accepting Invitations

The invited developer must:

1. Log in to GitHub.
2. Open their notifications or invitation email.
3. Review the organization invitation.
4. Accept the invitation.

After acceptance, the developer becomes an organization member.

## 4. Pending Invitations

A pending invitation is an invitation that has been sent but not yet accepted.

Organization owners can review pending invitations and cancel invitations when necessary.

## 5. Removing Members

To remove an organization member:

1. Open the organization.
2. Navigate to People.
3. Find the member.
4. Open the member management options.
5. Select Remove from organization.
6. Confirm the action.

Removing a member does not delete their personal GitHub account.

## 6. Organization Members vs Outside Collaborators

| Organization Members | Outside Collaborators |
|---|---|
| Belong to the organization | Do not become organization members |
| Can access resources according to assigned permissions | Receive access to specifically granted resources |
| Can participate in organization teams | Do not receive normal organization membership through repository access |

Outside collaborators are useful when external developers need access to selected repositories without joining the organization.

## Practical Exercise

1. Open your practice organization.
2. Navigate to People.
3. Invite a trusted developer.
4. Ask the developer to accept the invitation.
5. Verify their membership.
6. Explore pending invitation management.
7. Examine member removal options.

## Key Takeaways

- Organization owners can invite and manage members.
- Invitations must be accepted before membership becomes active.
- Pending invitations have not yet been accepted.
- Members and outside collaborators have different access arrangements.
- Removing a member does not delete their personal GitHub account.
